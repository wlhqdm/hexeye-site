HexEye

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 16, 2026

Overview

HexEye (海克斯眼) is a local Windows helper for League of Legends Hextech Mayhem. It runs on your PC and is designed to minimize data leaving your device.

Data we process locally

  • League Client (LCU) and Live Client Data endpoints on your machine, read-only, for game phase / level / bench context
  • Optional short screen captures for OCR fallback when game-event access is unavailable
  • Local logs under your temp directory for debugging (e.g. recognition status)

What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal data
  • We do not require a HexEye cloud account
  • We do not collect other players’ private information for undeclared purposes

Third parties

If you enable Overwolf Game Events Provider (when approved), event data is processed under Overwolf’s and Riot’s applicable terms. Static rating tables may be bundled with the app from public community data sources.
